Privacy Policy

NeverLate · Last updated: 17 June 2026

The short version

  • Your events, plans and reminders live on your device. We don't keep a copy on our servers.
  • We never sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites for advertising.
  • A few features need to send specific data to trusted service providers to work — for example, scanning an image to pull out an event, or working out your travel time. Details below.
  • NeverLate has optional paid plans. Payments are handled by Apple, so we never see your card or payment details.
  • There is no account and no sign-up. Delete the app and the data on your device goes with it.

This policy explains what NeverLate (“the app”, “we”) does with your information. This app is made by NeverLate. If anything here is unclear, email us at neverlatesupport@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Almost everything. The events you add, the calendar entries you import, your timelines, reminders and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not upload them to a NeverLate account, because there is no NeverLate account.

Calendar access

If you allow it, NeverLate reads events from your device calendars so it can show and plan around them. This access is read-only — we don't change or delete your calendar events, and the calendar data we read stays on your device.

Where some data goes, and why

To make certain features work, the app sends limited data to the service providers below. Each is used only for the purpose listed.

  • Scanning an image for an event — when you add an event from a photo or screenshot, that image is sent to our event-extraction provider (Google Gemini) to detect the date, time, place and other details. Where possible, parts of this happen on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition. Images are processed to read the event and are not used to build an advertising profile.
  • Travel times, directions and parking — when you plan a trip, your location and the relevant addresses are sent to Google Maps / Directions to estimate how long it takes to get there and to find nearby parking.
  • Backend processing — some of the above is routed through Firebase (Google) cloud services that we use to run the app's processing.
  • Usage analytics — we use PostHog (hosted in the EU) to understand how the app is used — which screens are opened, which features are used — so we can improve it. This includes recording a masked playback of how you move through the app (taps and screen flow): your content is hidden on your device before anything is sent, so these recordings never show your event details, addresses, times or photos. This is our own product analytics, not advertising tracking.
  • Subscriptions and purchases — NeverLate offers optional paid plans (monthly, yearly, or a one-time Lifetime purchase). Payments are handled entirely by Apple's App Store, so we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check and manage your subscription status. RevenueCat receives the App Store purchase record (what was bought and when), linked to a random, app-generated ID — not your name, email or Apple Account — and we use it only to unlock and restore the features you've paid for.

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies: Google, Firebase, PostHog, RevenueCat.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Calendar — to read and plan around your existing events (read-only).
  • Location — to work out travel time from where you are and find parking. Used when you're planning a trip.
  • Photos — only for images you choose to scan into an event.
  • Notifications & alarms — to remind you at the right moments, including must-not-miss alarms you set yourself.

You can change any of these any time in the iOS Settings app. If you turn one off, the related feature simply won't run — the rest of the app keeps working.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.
  • We don't require an account, a login, or your email to use the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app. Information sent to the providers above is kept only as long as needed to provide the feature, under their policies. To ask about analytics data we hold, email neverlatesupport@gmail.com.

Children

NeverLate is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the “last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email neverlatesupport@gmail.com.
